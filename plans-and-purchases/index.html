Security and governance

Purchases stay auditable.

Why checkout is hosted in the portal

The portal owns learner identity, Microsoft sign-in, payment transactions, and entitlement records. This page explains and routes purchases; the portal performs secure checkout.

How access is granted

Browser redirects do not grant access. Access is issued only after a verified PayFast ITN or PayPal webhook updates the entitlement record.
