Subscription flow

How recurring checkout works.

1

Select plan

Choose Starter, Pro, Mentor, or a Team Seat.

2

Choose gateway

Use PayFast for ZAR or PayPal for USD recurring billing.

3

Approve securely

PayPal uses its JavaScript SDK and Billing Subscriptions API; PayFast hosts its recurring checkout.

4

Activate access

Signed webhooks update the transaction and create or renew the portal entitlement.

Security and governance

Subscriptions stay auditable.

Why checkout is hosted in the portal

The portal owns learner identity, payment transactions, PayPal subscription references, and entitlement lifecycle records. This page explains and routes purchases; the portal performs secure checkout.

How recurring access is controlled

Browser redirects never grant access. Verified activation and payment webhooks activate or renew access, while cancellation, suspension, expiration, refund, and reversal events update the entitlement state.
