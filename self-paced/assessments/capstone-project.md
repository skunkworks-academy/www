# CLD-UF-101 Capstone Project

**Assessment weight:** 40% of final self-paced score  
**Purpose:** Demonstrate practical Claude use on a real or realistic work task.

---

## Capstone Brief

Complete one work-related task using Claude. Your task should be useful, realistic, and safe to perform in an online course environment.

You must show that you can:

1. Choose a suitable task.
2. Select the right use case.
3. Write a strong prompt.
4. Refine the output with at least one follow-up prompt.
5. Use a document or file if appropriate and allowed.
6. Identify what must be verified before using the result.
7. Explain how the result applies to real work.

---

## Task Options

Choose one of the following or propose your own equivalent task.

### Option A — Draft a Workplace Communication

Examples:

- Learner reminder email
- Internal project update
- Client follow-up message
- Meeting invitation text
- Change announcement

### Option B — Summarize and Structure Information

Examples:

- Meeting notes to action items
- Report to executive summary
- Policy to checklist
- Email thread to decisions and risks

### Option C — Brainstorm and Evaluate Ideas

Examples:

- Campaign ideas
- Process improvements
- Training activities
- Customer engagement ideas
- Risk mitigations

### Option D — Analyse Options

Examples:

- Compare vendors
- Prioritize initiatives
- Evaluate delivery models
- Build a decision matrix

### Option E — Explain a Concept

Examples:

- Explain an AI concept to a non-technical audience
- Prepare a briefing for a manager
- Create an FAQ for learners

### Option F — Work with a File

Examples:

- Turn a safe document into a checklist
- Summarize a report
- Extract requirements from a policy
- Analyse a spreadsheet or table

---

## Capstone Rules

- Do not submit confidential or sensitive data.
- Use anonymized, public, sample, or approved content.
- Do not rely on Claude as the final authority for high-stakes work.
- Include at least one follow-up prompt.
- Include a verification list.
- Explain how the final output would be used.

---

## Completion Standard

A successful capstone demonstrates:

- Clear task selection
- Appropriate use-case selection
- Strong prompt construction
- Meaningful iteration
- Safe document/data handling
- Practical verification plan
- Clear workplace transfer
