Evidence-Based Slide Design for Digital Learning

Learn to architect, build, test and publish slide-based learning that is cognitively efficient, visually clear, interactive, accessible and measurable—not merely attractive.

Capability outcomes

From slide formatting to learning-product engineering.

Learning architecture

Translate measurable outcomes into aligned evidence, practice, assessment and a deliberate slide sequence.

Cognitive design

Apply cognitive-load management, chunking, signaling, contiguity, segmenting and multimedia principles deliberately.

Visual explanation

Build typography, hierarchy, diagrams, process visuals, comparisons, data graphics and progressive disclosures that teach.

Interaction and feedback

Create retrieval practice, scenarios, classification, branching, guided notes and feedback that require meaningful thinking.

Accessibility

Design titles, reading order, alt text, captions, keyboard operation, non-colour cues and accessible interactions from the storyboard stage.

Publishing and measurement

Plan SCORM/xAPI tracking, LMS completion rules, analytics questions, QA and production validation.

Practical evidence

Eight build labs plus a complete capstone module.

Labs cover slide diagnosis, outcome architecture, cognitive-load refactoring, storyboarding, visual teaching sequences, interactive prototypes, accessibility remediation and LMS publishing/tracking. The capstone requires the learner to produce a complete accessible learning module and evidence portfolio.
