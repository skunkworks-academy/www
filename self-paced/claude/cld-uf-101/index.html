Claude User Fundamentals

A practical course that takes you from “what is Claude, exactly?” to confidently using it on real work: drafting, summarizing, brainstorming, analysis, document workflows, prompting, and responsible AI use. No coding, no jargon, no prior AI experience.

Learning objectives

By the end of this course, participants will be able to:

01Explain what Claude is and where its limitations are.
02Start and manage conversations across Claude interfaces.
03Apply Claude to writing, summarization, brainstorming, analysis, and Q&A.
04Write clear, specific prompts that produce better results.
05Upload and work with approved documents and files.
06Use Projects, instructions, and Memory features appropriately.
07Identify when AI output should be verified or used with caution.
08Apply these skills to a real task from the learner's role.

Assessment & certification

A practice-based approach: learners demonstrate that they can use Claude responsibly and verify outputs.

Knowledge quiz 40%

A randomized check on Claude capabilities, limits, prompting, and best practices.

Capstone review 60%

A real or realistic task showing prompt, iteration, output, and verification plan.
