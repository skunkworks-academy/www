Skunkworks AI Training Model

A repeatable model for learning design, readiness and capability evidence.

Our AI training model helps learners and teams move from unclear goals to structured learning, practical activities, measurable readiness and evidence that supports badges, certification or workplace capability.

1. Diagnose

Identify the learner role, target certification, current skill level, constraints, preferred pace and required outcome.

2. Design

Generate a structured learning path with modules, study blocks, practice tasks, labs, revision cycles and checkpoints.

3. Deliver

Use self-paced material, AI-supported coaching, facilitator reviews, portal access and guided practice resources.

4. Validate

Measure readiness through quizzes, assignments, capstones, reflection, evidence review and practice exam milestones.

5. Badge

Prepare for digital badges, vendor certification, internal recognition or documented evidence of skills acquisition.

6. Improve

Use performance data and learner feedback to adapt the path, close gaps and prepare for the next certification or role level.
