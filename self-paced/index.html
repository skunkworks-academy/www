Skunkworks AI Training Model

A repeatable model for learning design, readiness and capability evidence.

Our AI training model helps learners and teams move from unclear goals to structured learning, practical activities, measurable readiness and evidence that supports badges, certification or workplace capability.

1. Diagnose

Identify role, target certification, current skill level, constraints, pace and outcome.

2. Design

Generate a structured path with modules, study blocks, labs, revision cycles and checkpoints.

3. Deliver

Use self-paced material, AI coaching, facilitator reviews, portal access and practice resources.

4. Validate

Measure readiness through quizzes, assignments, capstones and practice exam milestones.

5. Badge

Prepare for digital badges, vendor certification or documented workplace skills evidence.

6. Improve

Use performance data and learner feedback to adapt the path and close gaps.
