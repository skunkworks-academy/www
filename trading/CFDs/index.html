Course overview

Understand contracts for difference, margin, leverage, spreads and the risks of trading leveraged products.

This short course is designed for learners who want a structured introduction with practical examples, guided activities and self-checks. The interactive lessons and assessments are currently being developed.

What you'll learn

  1. What is a CFD?
  2. Underlying markets and contract specifications
  3. Bid/ask spreads, commissions and overnight financing
  4. Margin, leverage and liquidation risk
  5. Worked trade examples and a risk checklist
