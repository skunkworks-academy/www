Cookie Policy

Effective and last reviewed: 7 August 2026

What cookies and local storage are

Cookies and local-storage records are small data items used by websites to remember preferences, maintain sessions, measure usage or support advertising. Similar technologies include web beacons, pixels and device identifiers.

Essential storage

Essential storage supports security, authentication, session continuity, routing, accessibility preferences and consent records. It may be used without optional advertising consent where legally permitted because the requested service cannot operate reliably without it.

Optional analytics

Analytics may be used to understand page performance, navigation and content usefulness. Where consent is required, optional analytics is not activated until consent is recorded.

Optional advertising

Advertising cookies and Google advertising code are restricted to eligible publisher-content pages and are loaded only after the applicable consent choice. They are not intentionally loaded on login, registration, checkout, account, administrative, form-only, error or laboratory-console pages.

Your choice

The site consent banner provides an option to accept optional cookies or use essential storage only. A consent record is stored in your browser under skunkworks-academy-cookie-consent-v1. You can reset your choice by clearing site data in your browser.

Browser and provider controls

Browsers allow cookies and site data to be viewed, blocked or deleted. Blocking essential storage may affect service functionality. Google also provides controls for advertising personalisation through its own account and advertising settings.

Changes

Cookie categories and providers may change as services evolve. Material changes will be reflected in this policy and its review date.
