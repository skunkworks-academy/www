About Skunkworks Academy

A practitioner-led technology learning publisher and delivery platform based in Johannesburg, South Africa.

What we publish

Skunkworks Academy publishes original technical guides, implementation checklists, learning-path analysis, hands-on laboratory guidance and structured course information. Our primary subject areas are cloud computing, cybersecurity, artificial intelligence, APIs and integration, enterprise infrastructure, automation and professional technology capability.

How the Academy differs from a course directory

Course listings explain what is available. Our editorial content explains why a capability matters, how to plan for it, which controls and prerequisites are required, what practical evidence should be produced and how learning should connect to operational outcomes. We treat learning as part of an implementation system rather than an isolated attendance event.

Who the content is for

The content is written for technology professionals, graduates, instructors, delivery teams, managers, partner organisations and decision-makers evaluating technical capability programmes. Some resources are suitable for individual learners; others are designed for organisations planning multi-role training, lab environments or certification pathways.

Editorial leadership

The editorial programme is led by Raydo Matthee, an IBM Champion, IBM Redbooks Gold Author, technical instructor and solutions architect. Specialist material may be reviewed by additional instructors or subject-matter experts. Named authors, reviewers and review dates are shown where applicable.

Commercial relationships

Skunkworks Academy provides paid training, labs, certification preparation and technology services. We may also use advertising or affiliate links on eligible editorial pages. Commercial relationships do not determine technical conclusions. Advertising and affiliate relationships are disclosed and kept separate from authentication, checkout, forms, learner administration and lab-console interfaces.

Contact and corrections

General enquiries can be sent to training@skunkworks.africa. Corrections and editorial concerns can be sent to editorial@skunkworks.africa.
