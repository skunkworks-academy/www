4. Data Governance & Privacy

You agree to handle data responsibly and in line with applicable governance, privacy and compliance obligations. Users must:

  • Store only approved data within the environment.
  • Respect Data Loss Prevention (DLP) policies and connector restrictions.
  • Avoid uploading sensitive personal information unless expressly authorized.
  • Follow applicable regional compliance requirements, including South Africa POPIA and relevant Qatar data protection requirements where applicable.
  • Use approved storage, Dataverse, SharePoint, OneDrive, Teams and connector locations only where permitted by policy.

All activity may be logged for security, compliance, auditability and operational integrity.

5. Security Requirements

Users must apply secure development and operational practices throughout the maker lifecycle. Users are required to:

  • Follow role-based access controls and least-privilege access principles.
  • Build only within managed or administrator-approved solutions where required.
  • Maintain secure development practices for apps, flows, connectors, credentials and shared resources.
  • Protect secrets, tokens, keys, credentials and connection references from unauthorized exposure.
  • Report suspicious activity, unauthorized access, data exposure or suspected compromise immediately.

Violations may result in suspension, permanent removal of access, administrative remediation or additional corrective action.

Monitoring is performed to support security, compliance, operational quality, service continuity and governance assurance.
