How the pathway works

1. Explore

Choose a course on a phone, tablet or computer. No equipment purchase is necessary for theory lessons.

2. Practise thinking

Use safe simulations, paper-based calculations, quizzes and work scenarios. Never attempt unsupervised high-risk work.

3. Show learning

Finish the modules and pass the knowledge check to print a clearly labelled, non-accredited learning record.

4. Find a pathway

Explore apprenticeships, employment support and future practical programmes through approved providers.

Careers and progression

Explore apprenticeship and entry-level career-search resources, understand what employers may ask for, and prepare for future hands-on training with an authorised skills development provider.
