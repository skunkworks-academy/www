# Mentor and review framework

Ammar requested mentoring, especially around programming, cybersecurity and AI programming.

## Mentor profile
The best mentor is someone who can review code, explain engineering trade-offs and connect security principles to practical application development.

## Cadence
- **Fortnightly:** 30-minute progress review.
- **Monthly:** code/project review.
- **Quarterly:** roadmap and specialisation decision.

## Review questions
- What did you build?
- What failed, and what did you learn?
- What evidence proves the new capability?
- Which concept can you now explain without notes?
- What is the highest-value gap for the next two weeks?
