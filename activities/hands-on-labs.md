# CLD-UF-101 Hands-On Activities

These activities are designed for live instructor-led delivery. Learners should use real or realistic workplace tasks wherever possible.

---

## Lab 1 — Send Your First Claude Message

**Aligned Unit:** Unit 2 — Getting Started  
**Time:** 10 minutes

### Objective

Start a Claude conversation and understand that prompting is natural-language interaction.

### Instructions

1. Open Claude through web, desktop, or mobile.
2. Start a brand-new conversation.
3. Ask Claude how an AI assistant could help someone in your job role.
4. Ask one follow-up question to make the answer more relevant.

### Evidence of Completion

Learner can show the instructor their prompt and one follow-up response.

---

## Lab 2 — Apply One Core Use Case

**Aligned Unit:** Unit 3 — Core Use Cases  
**Time:** 15 minutes

### Objective

Use Claude for one real task in one of the five core use cases.

### Choose One Track

1. **Writing and Editing:** draft or improve a message, report section, proposal paragraph, or announcement.
2. **Summarization:** condense a long document, notes, or thread into decisions, risks, and actions.
3. **Brainstorming:** generate options and group them by effort and impact.
4. **Analysis:** compare options using criteria such as cost, time, risk, and expected value.
5. **Ask and Explain:** explain a concept for a selected audience at the right depth.

### Evidence of Completion

Learner can explain which use case they selected and why.

---

## Lab 3 — Improve a Prompt

**Aligned Unit:** Unit 4 — Effective Prompting  
**Time:** 12 minutes

### Objective

Experience the difference between a vague prompt and a specific prompt.

### Instructions

1. Choose a prompt from Lab 2.
2. Rewrite it to include audience, purpose, context, output format, length, tone, and constraints.
3. Run both prompts.
4. Compare the quality of the answers.

### Reflection Questions

- What changed in the output?
- Which details improved the result most?
- What would you add next time?

### Evidence of Completion

Learner can show before/after prompt results.

---

## Lab 4 — Work with a Document or File

**Aligned Unit:** Unit 5 — Working with Documents and Files  
**Time:** 12 minutes

### Objective

Use Claude with learner-provided material.

### Instructions

1. Upload a document, spreadsheet, PDF, image, or slide deck that is appropriate to share.
2. Ask Claude to summarize it.
3. Ask one specific question about the contents.
4. Ask Claude to turn one part of the answer into a reusable format such as a table, checklist, or email.

### Evidence of Completion

Learner can show the document-based question and Claude’s answer.

---

## Lab 5 — Build a Verification List

**Aligned Unit:** Unit 7 — Best Practices and Pitfalls  
**Time:** 8 minutes

### Objective

Identify where Claude output must be checked before use.

### Instructions

Create a short personal verification list for your role. Examples include customer-facing claims, financial figures, legal or compliance statements, dates, names, citations, and sensitive data.

### Evidence of Completion

Learner can explain at least three verification categories relevant to their job.

---

## Final Lab — Complete One Real Task End to End

**Aligned Unit:** Unit 8 — Hands-On Practice and Q&A  
**Time:** 18 minutes

### Objective

Use the full course workflow on one real task.

### Instructions

1. Pick one real task from your work.
2. Choose the correct use case.
3. Write a clear prompt with context, audience, output format, tone, length, and constraints.
4. Upload a file if useful and allowed.
5. Iterate on Claude’s response at least once.
6. Identify what must be verified before using the output.
7. Explain how you would apply the result.

### Evidence of Completion

Instructor reviews the learner’s final task using the lab rubric.
