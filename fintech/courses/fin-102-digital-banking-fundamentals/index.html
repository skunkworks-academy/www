Digital Banking Fundamentals

Learning outcomes

  • Explain core concepts and terminology in digital banking fundamentals.
  • Map a realistic financial-services workflow with stakeholders, information flows and risks.
  • Apply privacy, cybersecurity and operational controls.
  • Complete an evidence-based practical activity and competency check.

Practical lab

Using synthetic financial-services records and authorised sandbox environments, model a representative digital banking fundamentals workflow, document security and regulatory controls, then submit a short report and supporting evidence.

Assessment

Knowledge checks and practical evidence submission. A Skunkworks Academy certificate of completion may be awarded after internal assessment. No statutory authorisation, professional designation or vendor endorsement is claimed.

South African context

Where applicable, review guidance from SARB, the Prudential Authority, FSCA, FIC, NCR and the Information Regulator. Applicability depends on the activity; verify current official requirements before delivery.

Educational content only; not financial, investment or legal advice. Use simulated data and authorised testing systems.
