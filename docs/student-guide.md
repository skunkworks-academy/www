# CLD-UF-101 — Claude User Fundamentals
## Student Course Guide

**Course Code:** CLD-UF-101  
**Level / Track:** Foundational — Level 1  
**Duration:** Half-day, approximately 3 hours 50 minutes including breaks  
**Delivery Mode:** Instructor-led, in-person or live virtual  
**Target Audience:** Knowledge workers and business users with no technical background required  
**Prerequisites:** None — basic computer and internet literacy only

---

## 1. Welcome and Course Overview

Welcome to **Claude User Fundamentals**. This course is designed for learners who are new to Claude or who have used AI assistants informally but want a stronger, safer, and more productive foundation.

Across one focused session, you will move from “What is Claude?” to applying it to real work: drafting, summarizing, brainstorming, analysing, working with files, improving prompts, and knowing when to verify output.

The emphasis is practical: you will not only learn about Claude, you will use it on a task from your own role.

---

## 2. Learning Outcomes

By the end of this course, you will be able to:

1. Explain what Claude is and how it differs from a search engine.
2. Start and manage conversations in Claude’s web, desktop, or mobile interfaces.
3. Apply Claude to writing, summarization, brainstorming, analysis, and Q&A.
4. Improve prompt quality using audience, context, tone, length, format, examples, and constraints.
5. Upload and work with documents, spreadsheets, PDFs, images, and slide content.
6. Use Artifacts for substantial reusable outputs.
7. Understand Projects, custom instructions, styles, and optional Memory.
8. Identify when Claude output needs verification, especially for high-stakes tasks.
9. Complete one real work task using Claude and explain how you checked or improved the result.

---

## 3. Course Modules

### Module 1: Introduction to Claude — 20 minutes

**Description:** What Claude is, how it differs from a search engine, and what it can and cannot do.

**Key Topics**

- Claude as an AI assistant
- Knowledge cutoffs
- Optional web search
- Capabilities and limitations
- Claude as a tool to augment judgement

**Activity:** Group discussion on prior AI experience.

---

### Module 2: Getting Started — 25 minutes

**Description:** Orientation to Claude’s interfaces and how conversations work.

**Key Topics**

- Web, desktop, and mobile access
- Starting a new conversation
- Conversation context within one thread
- Model tiers at a high level

**Activity:** Log in, start a new conversation, and ask Claude an open-ended question about your own job role.

---

### Module 3: Core Use Cases — 40 minutes

**Description:** The five most common ways people use Claude at work.

**Key Topics**

- Writing and editing
- Summarization
- Brainstorming
- Analysis
- Ask and explain / Q&A

**Activity:** Choose one use case and complete a real or realistic task.

---

### Break — 10 minutes

---

### Module 4: Effective Prompting — 35 minutes

**Description:** How to ask for what you actually want.

**Key Topics**

- Be clear and specific
- Provide context
- Give examples
- Iterate
- Ask Claude to ask clarifying questions

**Activity:** Revise one of your earlier prompts and compare the old and improved results.

---

### Module 5: Working with Documents and Files — 30 minutes

**Description:** How to bring your own content into Claude.

**Key Topics**

- Uploading PDFs, Word documents, spreadsheets, slides, images, and text files
- Asking questions about uploaded content
- Extracting key data
- Finding inconsistencies
- Rewriting sections
- Using Artifacts for reusable outputs

**Activity:** Upload a real document, ask Claude to summarize it, then ask one specific question about its contents.

---

### Break — 10 minutes

---

### Module 6: Projects and Memory — 25 minutes

**Description:** Features that help Claude support repeated work.

**Key Topics**

- Projects as dedicated workspaces with shared context
- Custom instructions and styles
- Optional Memory
- User control over remembered details

**Activity:** Discuss recurring tasks that could benefit from saved context.

---

### Module 7: Best Practices and Pitfalls — 25 minutes

**Description:** How to use Claude responsibly and avoid common mistakes.

**Key Topics**

- Claude can be confidently wrong
- Verify high-stakes output
- Protect sensitive and confidential information
- Use Claude for drafts, not final sign-off

**Activity:** Build a short personal verification list for your own work.

---

### Module 8: Hands-On Practice and Q&A — 30 minutes

**Description:** Apply the full course to one real task.

**Key Topics**

- Independent practice
- Instructor support
- Open Q&A
- Personal action commitment

**Activity:** Complete a real work task end to end and name one thing you will try with Claude this week.

---

## 4. Student Requirements

Before the session, make sure you have:

- A laptop or device with internet access
- Access to Claude through the web, desktop app, or mobile app
- One real work document, email, or task you are allowed to use for practice
- Permission to use any document you upload, especially if it contains company, client, or personal information

---

## 5. Assessment and Completion

This course uses a lightweight, practice-based assessment model.

| Component | Weight | Description |
|---|---:|---|
| Knowledge quiz | 40% | Short quiz on core concepts, prompting, documents/files, Projects/Memory, and responsible use |
| Hands-on lab review | 60% | Instructor review of the real task completed during Module 8 |

**Passing criteria:** 70% or higher combined, with a completed and reviewed lab activity.

**Retake policy:** Students who do not pass may retake the quiz once and resubmit a lab activity within 2 weeks.

---

## 6. Certificate of Completion

To earn the **CLD-UF-101 Certificate of Completion**, you must:

1. Attend the full course.
2. Complete the knowledge quiz.
3. Complete the Module 8 hands-on lab activity.
4. Achieve a combined score of 70% or higher.

Certificates are normally issued within 5 business days after successful completion.
