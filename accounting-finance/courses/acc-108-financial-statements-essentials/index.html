Financial Statements Essentials

Learning outcomes

  • Explain the core concepts and professional terminology of financial statements essentials
  • Complete an end-to-end workplace exercise using realistic South African business scenarios
  • Review outputs for accuracy, traceability, ethical practice and internal-control compliance

Practical lab

Use a simulated South African SME to process source documents, calculate or reconcile relevant figures and submit a short evidence-based report.

Assessment and completion

Knowledge checks, practical task submission and instructor or automated review. A Skunkworks Academy certificate of completion may be issued following successful internal assessment; this is not a professional designation or third-party accreditation.

Who should attend?

Accountants and SMEs. No formal prerequisite unless a facilitator recommends previous finance experience.

Training status: proposed proprietary educational course. No SAICA, SAIPA, IRBA, CIMA, ACCA or software-vendor endorsement or accreditation is claimed. For tax and statutory topics, validate current official guidance before delivery.
