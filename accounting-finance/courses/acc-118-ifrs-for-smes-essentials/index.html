IFRS for SMEs Essentials

Practical 7h short course for sme accountants.

7hReportingSelf-paced or instructor-led

Who should attend?

SME accountants. Beginner-friendly orientation; experienced participants can use this as an applied refresher.

Learning outcomes

  • Describe the principles, terminology and applicable controls
  • Process practical sample data through an example workflow
  • Investigate errors and produce a clear audit trail
  • Complete a scenario-based competency assessment

Four-module learning plan

Module 1

Concepts and context

Describe the principles, terminology and applicable controls.

Module 2

Guided demonstration

Process practical sample data through an example workflow.

Module 3

Hands-on finance lab

Investigate errors and produce a clear audit trail.

Module 4

Validation and assessment

Complete a scenario-based competency assessment.

Practical activity

Work with a fictional South African SME and a supplied transaction dataset. Produce source documentation, calculations and an auditable reconciliation or report.

Assessment

Complete a knowledge check and submit a practical evidence package. Successful learners may receive a Skunkworks Academy certificate of completion, not a third-party professional qualification.

Course proposal: independently developed learning material. Not endorsed or accredited by a professional body or software vendor. Verify tax rules and software features at the time of delivery.
