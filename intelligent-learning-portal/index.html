Eight-step lifecycle

From role context to measurable readiness.

  1. Capture role and outcomes.Define the job, required capabilities, business context and target outcomes.
  2. Build the learner profile.Create or update the approved skills graph using current skills and evidence.
  3. Read the environment.Consider technology stack, policies, compliance needs, risk and learner preferences.
  4. Map outcomes to competencies.Translate job outcomes into teachable, assessable skill requirements.
  5. Select approved assets.Combine official vendor training, Skunkworks modules, labs, projects and assessments.
  6. Sequence the blended path.Arrange theory, practice, assessment and coaching in a logical learning route.
  7. Coach and intervene.The AI companion supports momentum while humans step in when risk, confidence or complexity requires it.
  8. Evidence and re-plan.Progress data, assessment evidence and manager signals refresh the path continuously.

Governed enterprise AI

The portal is an enterprise orchestration layer, not an ungoverned chatbot.

The agent operates under enterprise guardrails, approved sources, permissions and human escalation rules. This protects training quality, security, intellectual property and responsible AI governance.
