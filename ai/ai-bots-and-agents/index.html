Explain it simply

When someone says AI Bot, they usually mean an AI Chatbot. A chatbot is conversational. An AI Agent goes further by taking action across systems, workflows and APIs.

Frequently asked questions

Is an AI Bot the same as ChatGPT?

Not exactly. ChatGPT is a general AI assistant. An AI Bot is usually configured for a specific business, website, workflow, course catalogue or support function.

Can a chatbot use our own content?

Yes. It can be grounded in approved knowledge sources such as course outlines, schedules, FAQs, policies and service information.

When do we need an AI Agent?

Use an agent when the system must do work, such as creating tickets, updating a CRM, scheduling meetings or generating reports.

How do we keep it safe?

Use approved data, role-based access, audit logs, prompt guardrails, tool restrictions, escalation and human approval for high-impact actions.
