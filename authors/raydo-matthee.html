Author and reviewer

Raydo Matthee

IBM Champion, IBM Redbooks Gold Author, technical instructor and solutions architect.

Editorial role

Raydo leads the Skunkworks Academy editorial programme and is accountable for the scope, practical usefulness and review of published guidance across cloud, artificial intelligence, APIs and integration, cybersecurity, enterprise infrastructure, technical training and implementation-ready learning design.

Professional focus

His work connects technical architecture with capability development: defining role outcomes, sequencing learning, designing practical laboratories, establishing assessment evidence and helping teams move from certification knowledge into operational delivery.

Review principles

  • Separate sourced facts from practitioner judgement.
  • Prefer primary vendor documentation, standards and implementation evidence.
  • Identify security assumptions, permissions and lab boundaries.
  • State when guidance is architecture-specific or time-sensitive.
  • Review pages when platform, certification or policy changes materially affect the recommendation.

Subject review scope

Cloud and multi-cloud strategy, AI-enabled learning, APIs and integration, identity and zero trust, cybersecurity labs, IBM Power and LinuxONE, Microsoft cloud and productivity platforms, Asterisk and VoIP, technical course architecture, certification pathways and partner enablement.
