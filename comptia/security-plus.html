CompTIA Security+

CompTIA Security+ is the globally trusted entry‑level cybersecurity certification. It validates the hands‑on skills you need to secure networks, applications and data, and demonstrates your ability to identify and respond to security events.

Through our instructor‑led training and practice labs you will master foundational security concepts including network security, compliance, identity management, risk management and incident response. This certification is vendor‑neutral and widely recognized by employers worldwide.

Upon completion you will be prepared to take the Security+ (SY0‑601) exam and launch your career as a security analyst, junior penetration tester or IT auditor.
