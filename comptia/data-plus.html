CompTIA Data+

CompTIA Data+ demonstrates you have the skills to translate data into meaningful business insights. It’s ideal for professionals responsible for developing and promoting data‑driven decision making within their organizations.

This course teaches you to mine, analyze, interpret and visualize data using a variety of tools. You’ll learn to apply statistical methods, build reports and dashboards, and communicate findings effectively to stakeholders.

By the end you’ll be ready to take the Data+ (DA0‑001) exam and pursue roles in data analysis, business intelligence or reporting across industries.
