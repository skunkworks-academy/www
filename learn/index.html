How learning works

Discover. Practice. Prove. Apply.

A strong learning journey starts with the outcome you need and uses the right mix of instruction, practical work, assessment and support to reach it.

1. Discover

Choose a course, certification objective, learning path or capability area that matches your role and target outcome.

2. Practice

Use guided exercises, labs, scenarios and technical activities to turn concepts into repeatable skills.

3. Prove

Use assessments, project work and capability evidence to demonstrate what you can do rather than only what you completed.

Learning guidance

Find the right Skunkworks Academy learning option.

Where should I start?

Use All Courses when you already know the technology or skill you need. Use Learning Paths when you want a structured sequence built around a broader role or capability outcome.

Self-paced or instructor-led?

Choose self-paced learning for schedule flexibility and independent progress. Choose instructor-led delivery when live explanation, cohort interaction, facilitated labs or enterprise customisation are important.

Can training align to certification?

Yes. Microsoft, IBM, Cisco, CompTIA and security learning destinations provide vendor or certification-aligned routes where applicable, while the complete catalogue also includes broader technical capability programmes.
