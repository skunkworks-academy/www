Learning process

Move from discovery to evidence of capability.

Learning is most valuable when the learner can connect a concept to an action, environment or delivery outcome. Academy routes are therefore designed around progression rather than attendance alone.

1. Discover

Identify the technology, role, certification or operational capability you need to strengthen.

2. Learn

Use self-paced content or instructor-led delivery to build the required concepts and technical patterns.

3. Practice

Apply knowledge through labs, exercises, demonstrations, scenarios, assessments and implementation tasks.

4. Validate

Capture evidence, prepare for certification where relevant and continue into the next capability milestone.

Frequently asked questions

Technology learning at Skunkworks Academy

Can I learn at my own pace?

Yes. The self-paced catalogue provides flexible course discovery and learning routes. Public course overviews can be explored before enrolment, while protected lessons, labs and assessments are made available through authenticated learner access where required.

Do you provide instructor-led and private team training?

Yes. Instructor-led programmes can be delivered virtually, in a classroom or as a private cohort. Delivery can include demonstrations, practical labs, assessments, certification preparation and contextualisation for the technologies used by your organisation.

Can learning be aligned to Microsoft, IBM, Cisco or CompTIA technologies?

Yes. Dedicated Microsoft, IBM, Cisco and CompTIA learning hubs help learners and organisations discover platform-aligned and certification-focused options alongside the wider Skunkworks Academy catalogue.

Where do learners manage enrolment and progress?

The Skunkworks Academy learner portal is the primary route for account-based learning activity, enrolment, progress and other authenticated learner services as they are enabled across the Academy platform.
