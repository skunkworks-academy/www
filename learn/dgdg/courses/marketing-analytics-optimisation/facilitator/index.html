Facilitator and Assessor Notes

Marketing Analytics & Conversion

Pre-delivery quality checks

Assess learner needs, access accommodations, prerequisite competence and the business scenario. Use approved fictional data. Keep feedback respectful, specific, dated and traceable to course objectives.

Delivery and questioning plan

  1. Metric definitions and data governance

    Demonstrate: Qualified enquiries, booked calls, confirmed sales and opt-outs are measured per cohort rather than displayed as unlabelled totals.. Coaching prompt: how does the learner justify create kpi tree? Watch for: Mixing incompatible lead definitions.

  2. Cohort funnels and lag

    Demonstrate: January produces 120 enquiries, 30 qualified leads, 12 meetings and three customers: overall enquiry-to-customer rate is 2.5%.. Coaching prompt: how does the learner justify build cohort table? Watch for: Comparing unmatched acquisition and revenue windows.

  3. Tests, uncertainty and safety metrics

    Demonstrate: A produces 24 leads from 400 visits and B produces 32 from 400; observed rates are 6% versus 8%, but uncertainty remains.. Coaching prompt: how does the learner justify state hypothesis? Watch for: Calling a small sample definitive.

  4. Executive decisions and improvement

    Demonstrate: A quality decline on broad keywords suggests a controlled exclusion test with a two-week follow-up scorecard.. Coaching prompt: how does the learner justify draft insight narrative? Watch for: Publishing dashboards without any decision.

Answer key and rationale

  1. Useful metric specification includes: Correct option: Formula, source, owner and period. Review the module "Metric definitions and data governance": Start with a decision, then define required metrics, numerator, denominator, dates, owner and source. Explicit lineage and reconciliation protect management from contradictory dashboards.
  2. Three buyers from 120 enquiries is: Correct option: 2.5%. Review the module "Cohort funnels and lag": Cohorts share a start date or characteristic. Comparing matched groups can show conversion lag and reduce false conclusions from mixing different months.
  3. Strong experiment design: Correct option: Predefine outcomes and decision rule. Review the module "Tests, uncertainty and safety metrics": Form a falsifiable hypothesis and define outcome, eligibility, practical effect, sample plan and stop rules. An observed lift in a small sample is not guaranteed to persist.
  4. Actionable insight needs: Correct option: Finding, action, owner and next review. Review the module "Executive decisions and improvement": Translate a finding into a prioritised action, named accountable owner, date and verification method. Include alternatives, uncertainty and learner/customer consequences.
  5. Data lineage describes: Correct option: Origin and processing of reported figures. Apply the principles and controls in Marketing Analytics & Conversion; verify calculations and permissions before deciding.
  6. A guardrail metric tracks: Correct option: Potential adverse impact. Apply the principles and controls in Marketing Analytics & Conversion; verify calculations and permissions before deciding.
  7. If conversion rises but quality falls: Correct option: Investigate end-to-end value. Apply the principles and controls in Marketing Analytics & Conversion; verify calculations and permissions before deciding.
  8. A cohort is: Correct option: Group sharing a defined attribute or period. Apply the principles and controls in Marketing Analytics & Conversion; verify calculations and permissions before deciding.

Authenticity and lab marking

Review the learner's workbook and request a walkthrough. Ask the student to respond to a changed hypothetical scenario; note corrections and verify the original work. Score technical 30, evidence 25, governance 25 and communication 20. Flag critical personal-data disclosure or deceptive claims for remediation irrespective of average score.

Training service improvement

Record learner feedback, assessment problems, inaccessible materials, obsolete legal or vendor references, an owner for corrective action, and the verification date. Do not award an Academy credential through client-only practice quiz results.
