Study duration includes independent research, practical construction, review and corrective work. Foundation modules require no paid campaign accounts; practitioner and advanced entries benefit from previous DGDG units or equivalent competence.

Structured learning modules

Lesson 1 / 4

Metric definitions and data governance

Start with a decision, then define required metrics, numerator, denominator, dates, owner and source. Explicit lineage and reconciliation protect management from contradictory dashboards.

Lesson 2 / 4

Cohort funnels and lag

Cohorts share a start date or characteristic. Comparing matched groups can show conversion lag and reduce false conclusions from mixing different months.

Open lesson →
Lesson 3 / 4

Tests, uncertainty and safety metrics

Form a falsifiable hypothesis and define outcome, eligibility, practical effect, sample plan and stop rules. An observed lift in a small sample is not guaranteed to persist.

Lesson 4 / 4

Executive decisions and improvement

Translate a finding into a prioritised action, named accountable owner, date and verification method. Include alternatives, uncertainty and learner/customer consequences.

Assessment policy

Four guided lessons, a practice assessment and a practical evidence assignment support each course. An 80% quiz threshold is a formative readiness indicator, not a certificate. A qualified assessor reviews the project against technical accuracy (30%), reproducible evidence (25%), ethics, security and privacy (25%), and communication/improvement (20%). Proposed practical threshold: 70/100 and no critical deceptive claim, privacy or integrity failure. Learner identity and awards require a controlled LMS process.
