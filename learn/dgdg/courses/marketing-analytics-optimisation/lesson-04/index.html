DGDG-109 · Lesson 4/4

Executive decisions and improvement

Marketing Analytics & Conversion: Translate evidence into accountable actions

Lesson concept

Translate a finding into a prioritised action, named accountable owner, date and verification method. Include alternatives, uncertainty and learner/customer consequences.

Apply the principle to the stated business case before choosing tools or vendors. Define the relevant customer outcome, the information required to support a decision and a measurable way of validating the result. Avoid claims unsupported by evidence.

Worked example

A quality decline on broad keywords suggests a controlled exclusion test with a two-week follow-up scorecard.

Practical teaching sequence

  1. Step 1. Draft insight narrative. Record evidence, assumptions, and source provenance in the learner workbook.
  2. Step 2. Rank three options. Record evidence, assumptions, and source provenance in the learner workbook.
  3. Step 3. Assign accountable owners. Record evidence, assumptions, and source provenance in the learner workbook.
  4. Step 4. Set a review date. Record evidence, assumptions, and source provenance in the learner workbook.

For each step, include a brief rationale, an expected result and one potential validation method. When estimating marketing outcomes, label synthetic figures explicitly and distinguish estimates from actual observations.

Common misconception or risk: Publishing dashboards without any decision. Mitigation: review the evidence, customer impact, permissions and reporting definitions before release.

Formative checkpoint

Actionable insight needs:

Review the example, select the justified option in the end-of-course knowledge check, and explain why the other options fail the business or compliance objective.

Reflective assignment

Produce 150–250 words explaining what you designed, your highest-risk assumption, how you would verify it and what feedback would change your approach. Cite all external quantitative claims and mark fictional data as simulated.
