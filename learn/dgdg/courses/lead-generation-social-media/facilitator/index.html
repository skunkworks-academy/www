Facilitator and Assessor Notes

Lead Generation & Social Media

Pre-delivery quality checks

Assess learner needs, access accommodations, prerequisite competence and the business scenario. Use approved fictional data. Keep feedback respectful, specific, dated and traceable to course objectives.

Delivery and questioning plan

  1. Channel and audience selection

    Demonstrate: An IT training campaign tests LinkedIn and partner newsletters before allocating effort to entertainment-led channels.. Coaching prompt: how does the learner justify map audience media habits? Watch for: Trying every channel at once without evidence.

  2. Editorial planning and proof

    Demonstrate: An awareness checklist precedes a case example, buyer FAQ and webinar invitation over three weeks.. Coaching prompt: how does the learner justify define three content pillars? Watch for: Repeated clickbait and fabricated social proof.

  3. Lead forms, permission and routing

    Demonstrate: An optional subscription checkbox is separate from requesting a webinar seat; submissions receive a clear acknowledgement.. Coaching prompt: how does the learner justify sketch minimal form fields? Watch for: Prechecking subscription boxes without a lawful basis.

  4. Channel quality scorecards

    Demonstrate: Channel A gives 100 signups with five qualified; B gives 30 with 15 qualified. B has a 50% qualified rate.. Coaching prompt: how does the learner justify define qualifying criteria? Watch for: Reporting registrations as if all are valuable sales leads.

Answer key and rationale

  1. Best reason to choose a channel: Correct option: Its measured audience fit. Review the module "Channel and audience selection": Choose channels on buyer behaviour, search intent, format suitability, resources and policy. A platform with the most users is not necessarily the best channel for a particular B2B buyer.
  2. Appropriate awareness content is: Correct option: A practical checklist. Review the module "Editorial planning and proof": Match content to awareness, consideration and action. Provide a real educational takeaway, credible sources, captions and a clear next step. Use a measured frequency and named content owner.
  3. Best data-minimisation action: Correct option: Collect only needed fields. Review the module "Lead forms, permission and routing": Ask only for fields necessary to respond, explain their purpose and keep marketing preferences distinct where required. Route submissions by qualification and urgency, with clear ownership and service levels.
  4. Fifteen qualified among 30 leads is: Correct option: 50%. Review the module "Channel quality scorecards": Count qualified enquiries, booked calls, cost and downstream outcomes alongside signups. Watch sampling effects and avoid over-interpreting short periods.
  5. An SLA usually defines: Correct option: Agreed response time and owner. Apply the principles and controls in Lead Generation & Social Media; verify calculations and permissions before deciding.
  6. Segmentation helps: Correct option: Deliver relevant permitted follow-up. Apply the principles and controls in Lead Generation & Social Media; verify calculations and permissions before deciding.
  7. Many signups but few qualified indicates: Correct option: Possible poor audience or offer fit. Apply the principles and controls in Lead Generation & Social Media; verify calculations and permissions before deciding.
  8. An unsubscribe should trigger: Correct option: Appropriate suppression. Apply the principles and controls in Lead Generation & Social Media; verify calculations and permissions before deciding.

Authenticity and lab marking

Review the learner's workbook and request a walkthrough. Ask the student to respond to a changed hypothetical scenario; note corrections and verify the original work. Score technical 30, evidence 25, governance 25 and communication 20. Flag critical personal-data disclosure or deceptive claims for remediation irrespective of average score.

Training service improvement

Record learner feedback, assessment problems, inaccessible materials, obsolete legal or vendor references, an owner for corrective action, and the verification date. Do not award an Academy credential through client-only practice quiz results.
