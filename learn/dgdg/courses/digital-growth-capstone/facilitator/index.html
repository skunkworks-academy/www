Facilitator and Assessor Notes

Digital Growth Capstone Project

Pre-delivery quality checks

Assess learner needs, access accommodations, prerequisite competence and the business scenario. Use approved fictional data. Keep feedback respectful, specific, dated and traceable to course objectives.

Delivery and questioning plan

  1. Campaign scope and charter

    Demonstrate: A 60-day pilot aims for 30 qualified SME enquiries under a hypothetical R8,000 budget, without earnings promises.. Coaching prompt: how does the learner justify write mission and scope? Watch for: Beginning production without decision criteria.

  2. Integration architecture

    Demonstrate: An approved webinar post leads to a matching syllabus page, creates a deduplicated request and triggers a relevant acknowledgement.. Coaching prompt: how does the learner justify map data flow? Watch for: Building disconnected marketing artefacts.

  3. Scenario modelling and controls

    Demonstrate: At 1,000 visitors and 50 enquiries, simulated lead rate is 5%; a downside case has 25 enquiries, with controls to cap spending.. Coaching prompt: how does the learner justify model three scenarios? Watch for: Describing synthetic results as actual revenue.

  4. Portfolio defence and reflection

    Demonstrate: The assessor traces a campaign goal through audience research, tracking plan, conversion page, nurture, analytics and improvement backlog.. Coaching prompt: how does the learner justify assemble evidence? Watch for: Delivering attractive mockups without supporting evidence.

Answer key and rationale

  1. Required charter element: Correct option: Scope, metrics, owners and constraints. Review the module "Campaign scope and charter": A charter defines the buyer problem, audience, objective, owner, exclusions, budget, governance and measurable success. Document assumptions and approvals before production begins.
  2. A coherent funnel includes: Correct option: Acquisition, conversion, routing and measurement. Review the module "Integration architecture": Connect traffic sources, content, an accurate landing page, permission-aware form, CRM routing, nurture and reporting. Broken handoffs undermine both customer experience and attribution.
  3. Simulated outcomes must be called: Correct option: Modelled assumptions. Review the module "Scenario modelling and controls": Model best, expected and downside outcomes, including data failure, budget breach, opt-out errors and invalid events. Simulations are not observed commercial results.
  4. Competence is best demonstrated by: Correct option: Integrated traceable artefacts and reasoning. Review the module "Portfolio defence and reflection": Summative evidence must connect learning outcomes to artefacts, assumptions, calculations, ethical decisions and improvements. A reviewer should be able to reproduce central calculations and test the learner's reasoning.
  5. Portfolio traceability requires: Correct option: Evidence tied to outcomes and decisions. Apply the principles and controls in Digital Growth Capstone Project; verify calculations and permissions before deciding.
  6. Forecasts should be presented as: Correct option: Conditional scenarios with assumptions. Apply the principles and controls in Digital Growth Capstone Project; verify calculations and permissions before deciding.
  7. Which issue triggers a campaign stop? Correct option: Sending to unsubscribed contacts. Apply the principles and controls in Digital Growth Capstone Project; verify calculations and permissions before deciding.
  8. A capstone integrates: Correct option: All stages, risks and evaluation. Apply the principles and controls in Digital Growth Capstone Project; verify calculations and permissions before deciding.

Authenticity and lab marking

Review the learner's workbook and request a walkthrough. Ask the student to respond to a changed hypothetical scenario; note corrections and verify the original work. Score technical 30, evidence 25, governance 25 and communication 20. Flag critical personal-data disclosure or deceptive claims for remediation irrespective of average score.

Training service improvement

Record learner feedback, assessment problems, inaccessible materials, obsolete legal or vendor references, an owner for corrective action, and the verification date. Do not award an Academy credential through client-only practice quiz results.
