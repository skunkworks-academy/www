Facilitator and Assessor Notes

Landing Pages & Sales Funnels

Pre-delivery quality checks

Assess learner needs, access accommodations, prerequisite competence and the business scenario. Use approved fictional data. Keep feedback respectful, specific, dated and traceable to course objectives.

Delivery and questioning plan

  1. Mapping the conversion journey

    Demonstrate: A course comparison links to the syllabus, then a three-field enquiry form and a response-time confirmation.. Coaching prompt: how does the learner justify draw five funnel stages? Watch for: Using manipulative popups instead of genuine assistance.

  2. Page structure and trust signals

    Demonstrate: A course page shows skills, duration, prerequisites, delivery modes, trainer information and enquiry action.. Coaching prompt: how does the learner justify draft specific headline? Watch for: Overusing vague hype without tangible proof.

  3. Usable and lawful forms

    Demonstrate: Quote fields include company, work email and course of interest, with optional notes and a clear confirmation.. Coaching prompt: how does the learner justify design essential fields? Watch for: Bundling consent invisibly with the main submit action.

  4. Evidence-led optimisation

    Demonstrate: High mobile form starts but low submits suggest inspecting field layout before changing prices.. Coaching prompt: how does the learner justify choose a primary metric? Watch for: Treating small noisy tests as conclusive.

Answer key and rationale

  1. After a completed enquiry, provide: Correct option: A meaningful confirmation. Review the module "Mapping the conversion journey": Define the user objective at discovery, relevance, consideration, action and confirmation. Associate each stage with events, decisions and the information buyers need.
  2. Which heading describes a specific outcome? Correct option: Learn security fundamentals in six modules. Review the module "Page structure and trust signals": Use a descriptive heading, substantiated outcomes, honest prerequisites and pricing context, relevant testimonials where genuine, clear CTA and a consistent mobile experience.
  3. Accessible forms need: Correct option: Explicit labels and errors. Review the module "Usable and lawful forms": Labels, keyboard access, logical error handling and minimal data reduce friction. Clearly distinguish a requested quote from optional marketing communications.
  4. A relevant conversion guardrail is: Correct option: Qualified lead rate. Review the module "Evidence-led optimisation": Find abandonment points by device, stage and audience; formulate hypotheses; test variables and guard downstream quality. Tiny samples cannot establish certain gains.
  5. A clear CTA should: Correct option: Describe the next action. Apply the principles and controls in Landing Pages & Sales Funnels; verify calculations and permissions before deciding.
  6. Funnel drop-off means: Correct option: Users leaving before the next stage. Apply the principles and controls in Landing Pages & Sales Funnels; verify calculations and permissions before deciding.
  7. Form errors belong: Correct option: Near the affected field with accessible explanation. Apply the principles and controls in Landing Pages & Sales Funnels; verify calculations and permissions before deciding.
  8. Changing price and layout together makes: Correct option: Causal interpretation difficult. Apply the principles and controls in Landing Pages & Sales Funnels; verify calculations and permissions before deciding.

Authenticity and lab marking

Review the learner's workbook and request a walkthrough. Ask the student to respond to a changed hypothetical scenario; note corrections and verify the original work. Score technical 30, evidence 25, governance 25 and communication 20. Flag critical personal-data disclosure or deceptive claims for remediation irrespective of average score.

Training service improvement

Record learner feedback, assessment problems, inaccessible materials, obsolete legal or vendor references, an owner for corrective action, and the verification date. Do not award an Academy credential through client-only practice quiz results.
