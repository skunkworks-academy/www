Facilitator and Assessor Notes

Email Marketing & AI Automation

Pre-delivery quality checks

Assess learner needs, access accommodations, prerequisite competence and the business scenario. Use approved fictional data. Keep feedback respectful, specific, dated and traceable to course objectives.

Delivery and questioning plan

  1. List provenance and permitted purpose

    Demonstrate: A requested syllabus can be sent in response to an enquiry; a recurring promotion needs its own applicable justification.. Coaching prompt: how does the learner justify inventory contact sources? Watch for: Importing scraped or purchased contacts without adequate legal basis.

  2. Lifecycle design and frequency

    Demonstrate: Five messages include welcome guide, relevant case, decision checklist, webinar invitation and a final helpful follow-up.. Coaching prompt: how does the learner justify map five messages? Watch for: Continuing communication after opt-out.

  3. Automation and reliability

    Demonstrate: A webhook stores a request, checks eligibility, deduplicates by request ID, assigns an owner and sends an appropriate acknowledgement.. Coaching prompt: how does the learner justify diagram trigger and states? Watch for: Retries causing repeated unwanted emails.

  4. Responsible AI copy and analytics

    Demonstrate: A draft falsely claims international accreditation; a reviewer removes the claim unless independently substantiated.. Coaching prompt: how does the learner justify draft privacy-safe prompting rules? Watch for: Publishing hallucinated outcomes or awards.

Answer key and rationale

  1. Most useful email audit field: Correct option: Contact source and preference history. Review the module "List provenance and permitted purpose": Record why an address exists and which messages are appropriate. Explain uses, respect preferences, document regional marketing rules and separate requested responses from promotional campaigns where applicable.
  2. When should a nurture stop? Correct option: On opt-out or defined exit. Review the module "Lifecycle design and frequency": A nurture sequence should provide increasing value, not simply repeat sales requests. Define segmentation rules, wait periods, useful messages, handoff points and cancellation events.
  3. Why use an idempotency key? Correct option: Prevent duplicated processing. Review the module "Automation and reliability": Event-driven CRM workflows need validation, deduplication, idempotency, retries, monitoring, human escalation and scoped credentials. Do not embed secrets into student browser pages.
  4. Accountable publisher of AI marketing copy: Correct option: The organisation and reviewer. Review the module "Responsible AI copy and analytics": AI can assist with structure and variants, but humans must check claims, confidentiality, accessibility and intended audience. Monitor replies, complaints, qualified results and deliverability rather than relying solely on opens.
  5. A clearly requested service response is: Correct option: A syllabus requested in an enquiry. Apply the principles and controls in Email Marketing & AI Automation; verify calculations and permissions before deciding.
  6. Suppression lists help: Correct option: Prevent prohibited sends. Apply the principles and controls in Email Marketing & AI Automation; verify calculations and permissions before deciding.
  7. A harmful email signal is: Correct option: High complaint rate. Apply the principles and controls in Email Marketing & AI Automation; verify calculations and permissions before deciding.
  8. Before placing customer data in external AI tools: Correct option: Assess legal basis, approvals and controls. Apply the principles and controls in Email Marketing & AI Automation; verify calculations and permissions before deciding.

Authenticity and lab marking

Review the learner's workbook and request a walkthrough. Ask the student to respond to a changed hypothetical scenario; note corrections and verify the original work. Score technical 30, evidence 25, governance 25 and communication 20. Flag critical personal-data disclosure or deceptive claims for remediation irrespective of average score.

Training service improvement

Record learner feedback, assessment problems, inaccessible materials, obsolete legal or vendor references, an owner for corrective action, and the verification date. Do not award an Academy credential through client-only practice quiz results.
