DGDG-106 · Lesson 3/4

Automation and reliability

Email Marketing & AI Automation: Design reliable CRM workflow controls

Lesson concept

Event-driven CRM workflows need validation, deduplication, idempotency, retries, monitoring, human escalation and scoped credentials. Do not embed secrets into student browser pages.

Apply the principle to the stated business case before choosing tools or vendors. Define the relevant customer outcome, the information required to support a decision and a measurable way of validating the result. Avoid claims unsupported by evidence.

Worked example

A webhook stores a request, checks eligibility, deduplicates by request ID, assigns an owner and sends an appropriate acknowledgement.

Practical teaching sequence

  1. Step 1. Diagram trigger and states. Record evidence, assumptions, and source provenance in the learner workbook.
  2. Step 2. Specify deduplication key. Record evidence, assumptions, and source provenance in the learner workbook.
  3. Step 3. Create failure path. Record evidence, assumptions, and source provenance in the learner workbook.
  4. Step 4. Set escalation owner. Record evidence, assumptions, and source provenance in the learner workbook.

For each step, include a brief rationale, an expected result and one potential validation method. When estimating marketing outcomes, label synthetic figures explicitly and distinguish estimates from actual observations.

Common misconception or risk: Retries causing repeated unwanted emails. Mitigation: review the evidence, customer impact, permissions and reporting definitions before release.

Formative checkpoint

Why use an idempotency key?

Review the example, select the justified option in the end-of-course knowledge check, and explain why the other options fail the business or compliance objective.

Reflective assignment

Produce 150–250 words explaining what you designed, your highest-risk assumption, how you would verify it and what feedback would change your approach. Cite all external quantitative claims and mark fictional data as simulated.
