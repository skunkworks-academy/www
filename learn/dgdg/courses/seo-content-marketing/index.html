DGDG-108 · self-paced

SEO & Content Marketing

Develop helpful and discoverable content with accountable editorial processes.

DGDG-108Practitioner8 guided hours

Start courseApplied labWorkbook

Professional context

A training catalogue has excellent material but low relevant organic search visibility.

Measurable outcomes

  • Group audience questions by intent
  • Plan technical and on-page structure
  • Verify AI-assisted editorial output
  • Measure organic demand and content decay

Study duration includes independent research, practical construction, review and corrective work. Foundation modules require no paid campaign accounts; practitioner and advanced entries benefit from previous DGDG units or equivalent competence.

Structured learning modules

Lesson 1 / 4

Intent and topic clusters

Match informational, comparative and transactional user intent to genuinely helpful pages. Topic clusters connect a central subject with relevant supporting resources rather than repeating keywords.

Open lesson →
Lesson 2 / 4

On-page content architecture

Use descriptive titles, one main heading, semantic structure, accurate metadata, internal links and appropriate canonical URLs. Structured metadata must reflect what readers actually see.

Open lesson →
Lesson 3 / 4

Fact-checking and editorial governance

AI outputs can support research organisation and writing but claims, statistics, quotations and vendor steps require verification. Record author, source, revision and editorial sign-off.

Open lesson →
Lesson 4 / 4

Content distribution and maintenance

Distribute relevant resources through lawful owned and partner channels, track useful visits and qualified outcomes, and schedule updates after material tool or policy changes.

Assessment policy

Four guided lessons, a practice assessment and a practical evidence assignment support each course. An 80% quiz threshold is a formative readiness indicator, not a certificate. A qualified assessor reviews the project against technical accuracy (30%), reproducible evidence (25%), ethics, security and privacy (25%), and communication/improvement (20%). Proposed practical threshold: 70/100 and no critical deceptive claim, privacy or integrity failure. Learner identity and awards require a controlled LMS process.
