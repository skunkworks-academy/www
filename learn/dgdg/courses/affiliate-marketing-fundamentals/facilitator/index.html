Facilitator and Assessor Notes

Affiliate Marketing Fundamentals

Pre-delivery quality checks

Assess learner needs, access accommodations, prerequisite competence and the business scenario. Use approved fictional data. Keep feedback respectful, specific, dated and traceable to course objectives.

Delivery and questioning plan

  1. Affiliate roles and value exchange

    Demonstrate: For a R900 course with a 10% approved-sale rate, five eligible purchases yield R450 before fees, refunds and tax.. Coaching prompt: how does the learner justify draw the advertiser–publisher–buyer relationship? Watch for: Confusing referral visits with payable purchases.

  2. Customer journey and conversion math

    Demonstrate: 500 campaign clicks produce 50 enquiries and five eligible purchases: 10% enquiry rate per click and 1% purchase rate per click.. Coaching prompt: how does the learner justify draw seven journey stages? Watch for: Counting refunded orders as confirmed revenue.

  3. Offer evaluation and product integrity

    Demonstrate: Compare a 40% commission offer with unclear refund rules against a 12% offer with demonstrable value and clear support; weigh risk and fit.. Coaching prompt: how does the learner justify design weighted offer criteria? Watch for: Selecting only the highest payout.

  4. Promotion ethics and disclosures

    Demonstrate: A product guide notes that qualifying purchases through its disclosed referral link may result in commission.. Coaching prompt: how does the learner justify draft a visible affiliate disclosure? Watch for: Hiding a disclosure in a remote footer.

Answer key and rationale

  1. Who typically fulfills an affiliate offer? Correct option: Merchant. Review the module "Affiliate roles and value exchange": An advertiser or merchant fulfils an offer while a publisher introduces an interested audience. Referral attribution and programme terms determine eligibility for payment. Delivering value to the buyer comes before commission.
  2. Fifty enquiries from 500 clicks equals what lead rate? Correct option: 10%. Review the module "Customer journey and conversion math": Map discovery, click, evaluation, enquiry, purchase, fulfilment and possible reversal. Conversion definitions must specify the numerator, denominator, period and approval status; visits and sales are not interchangeable.
  3. Which selection factor is most defensible? Correct option: Audience problem and product fit. Review the module "Offer evaluation and product integrity": Assess offer suitability, buyer value, merchant support, promotion rules, refund policy, verifiable proof and reputation risk. A high commission can mask weak customer value and long-term brand cost.
  4. A commercial referral disclosure should be: Correct option: Clear and prominent. Review the module "Promotion ethics and disclosures": Material affiliate relationships should be clearly disclosed near a promotion, with evidence supporting each claim. Avoid fabricated endorsements, misleading scarcity and unsolicited acquisition of contact records.
  5. A refunded commission is best reported as: Correct option: Net eligible amount after applicable reversal. Apply the principles and controls in Affiliate Marketing Fundamentals; verify calculations and permissions before deciding.
  6. Which claim is highest risk without substantiation? Correct option: Guaranteed R10,000 earnings this week. Apply the principles and controls in Affiliate Marketing Fundamentals; verify calculations and permissions before deciding.
  7. Which measurement is closest to business value? Correct option: Qualified leads and eligible purchases. Apply the principles and controls in Affiliate Marketing Fundamentals; verify calculations and permissions before deciding.
  8. What should a seller do when the buyer asks about referral remuneration? Correct option: Provide an accurate explanation. Apply the principles and controls in Affiliate Marketing Fundamentals; verify calculations and permissions before deciding.

Authenticity and lab marking

Review the learner's workbook and request a walkthrough. Ask the student to respond to a changed hypothetical scenario; note corrections and verify the original work. Score technical 30, evidence 25, governance 25 and communication 20. Flag critical personal-data disclosure or deceptive claims for remediation irrespective of average score.

Training service improvement

Record learner feedback, assessment problems, inaccessible materials, obsolete legal or vendor references, an owner for corrective action, and the verification date. Do not award an Academy credential through client-only practice quiz results.
