Affiliate Marketing Fundamentals

Explain ethical affiliate models and plan a low-risk launch.

DGDG-101Foundation4 guided hours

Start courseApplied labWorkbook

Professional context

A Johannesburg course reseller wants a transparent referral programme without implying any earnings guarantee.

Measurable outcomes

  • Map the merchant, publisher and customer relationship
  • Calculate clicks, leads, sales and approved commissions
  • Compare offers for credibility and fit
  • Prepare disclosure and privacy checks

Study duration includes independent research, practical construction, review and corrective work. Foundation modules require no paid campaign accounts; practitioner and advanced entries benefit from previous DGDG units or equivalent competence.

Structured learning modules

Lesson 1 / 4

Affiliate roles and value exchange

An advertiser or merchant fulfils an offer while a publisher introduces an interested audience. Referral attribution and programme terms determine eligibility for payment. Delivering value to the buyer comes before commission.

Open lesson →
Lesson 2 / 4

Customer journey and conversion math

Map discovery, click, evaluation, enquiry, purchase, fulfilment and possible reversal. Conversion definitions must specify the numerator, denominator, period and approval status; visits and sales are not interchangeable.

Open lesson →
Lesson 3 / 4

Offer evaluation and product integrity

Assess offer suitability, buyer value, merchant support, promotion rules, refund policy, verifiable proof and reputation risk. A high commission can mask weak customer value and long-term brand cost.

Open lesson →
Lesson 4 / 4

Promotion ethics and disclosures

Material affiliate relationships should be clearly disclosed near a promotion, with evidence supporting each claim. Avoid fabricated endorsements, misleading scarcity and unsolicited acquisition of contact records.

Assessment policy

Four guided lessons, a practice assessment and a practical evidence assignment support each course. An 80% quiz threshold is a formative readiness indicator, not a certificate. A qualified assessor reviews the project against technical accuracy (30%), reproducible evidence (25%), ethics, security and privacy (25%), and communication/improvement (20%). Proposed practical threshold: 70/100 and no critical deceptive claim, privacy or integrity failure. Learner identity and awards require a controlled LMS process.
