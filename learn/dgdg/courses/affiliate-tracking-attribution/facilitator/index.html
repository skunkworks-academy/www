Facilitator and Assessor Notes

Affiliate Tracking & Attribution

Pre-delivery quality checks

Assess learner needs, access accommodations, prerequisite competence and the business scenario. Use approved fictional data. Keep feedback respectful, specific, dated and traceable to course objectives.

Delivery and questioning plan

  1. Events and URL taxonomy

    Demonstrate: utm_source=linkedin and utm_campaign=q4_webinar describe acquisition context but cannot validate a sale.. Coaching prompt: how does the learner justify list four events? Watch for: Placing customer emails inside URLs.

  2. Attribution models

    Demonstrate: Someone discovers a course on social media but returns via direct URL to purchase; last-touch may undercount the discovery source.. Coaching prompt: how does the learner justify sketch a multi-touch journey? Watch for: Assuming a model shows the entire customer journey.

  3. Reconciliation and ratios

    Demonstrate: 10,000 ad impressions generate 300 clicks, 30 enquiries, six approved purchases and one reversal. CTR is 3%; net eligible sales may be five.. Coaching prompt: how does the learner justify create a metric dictionary? Watch for: Mixing sessions, people and events within the same denominator.

  4. Consent, security and access

    Demonstrate: Affiliates can see aggregate conversion counts without access to unneeded individual contact records.. Coaching prompt: how does the learner justify build a data inventory? Watch for: Opening raw lead exports to every affiliate.

Answer key and rationale

  1. Which item belongs outside a tracking URL? Correct option: Customer email address. Review the module "Events and URL taxonomy": Events describe distinct behaviour such as landing view, qualified enquiry and approved purchase. UTM tags label acquisition source, medium and campaign; they should not include names, phone numbers or emails.
  2. Which model credits the first observed visit? Correct option: First-touch. Review the module "Attribution models": First-touch and last-touch reporting give credit to different interactions. Cookies, cross-device behaviour and consent restrictions limit observations. Attribution is a model, not proof of causation.
  3. 200 clicks from 10,000 impressions gives CTR of: Correct option: 2%. Review the module "Reconciliation and ratios": Use named denominators for click-through, lead and approved-sale rates. Distinguish gross events from net eligibility and record reporting windows, currencies and time-zone differences.
  4. Which is the safer dashboard? Correct option: Aggregated metrics with restricted detail. Review the module "Consent, security and access": Minimise personal data in campaign reporting, document consent-dependent technologies, define retention and make role-based dashboards the default. Apply least privilege to partner and payout access.
  5. Payable commissions should rely on: Correct option: Eligible approved transactions. Apply the principles and controls in Affiliate Tracking & Attribution; verify calculations and permissions before deciding.
  6. Analytics and sales ledgers can differ because of: Correct option: Attribution and reversals. Apply the principles and controls in Affiliate Tracking & Attribution; verify calculations and permissions before deciding.
  7. UTM source records: Correct option: Traffic origin label. Apply the principles and controls in Affiliate Tracking & Attribution; verify calculations and permissions before deciding.
  8. Traceability improves with: Correct option: Versioned definitions and owners. Apply the principles and controls in Affiliate Tracking & Attribution; verify calculations and permissions before deciding.

Authenticity and lab marking

Review the learner's workbook and request a walkthrough. Ask the student to respond to a changed hypothetical scenario; note corrections and verify the original work. Score technical 30, evidence 25, governance 25 and communication 20. Flag critical personal-data disclosure or deceptive claims for remediation irrespective of average score.

Training service improvement

Record learner feedback, assessment problems, inaccessible materials, obsolete legal or vendor references, an owner for corrective action, and the verification date. Do not award an Academy credential through client-only practice quiz results.
