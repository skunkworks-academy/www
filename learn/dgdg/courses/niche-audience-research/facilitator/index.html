Facilitator and Assessor Notes

Niche & Audience Research

Pre-delivery quality checks

Assess learner needs, access accommodations, prerequisite competence and the business scenario. Use approved fictional data. Keep feedback respectful, specific, dated and traceable to course objectives.

Delivery and questioning plan

  1. Niches, ICPs and personas

    Demonstrate: The niche is regional SMEs; the ICP has 20–100 staff and recurrent onboarding needs; the persona is a busy operations manager.. Coaching prompt: how does the learner justify write the niche boundaries? Watch for: Treating everyone as an eligible customer.

  2. Market research with source controls

    Demonstrate: Five interviews suggest demand for 90-minute awareness sessions but are too few to generalise across all South African SMEs.. Coaching prompt: how does the learner justify write neutral interview questions? Watch for: Inventing quotations and calling them interviews.

  3. Value propositions and buyer jobs

    Demonstrate: Reusable micro-learning and attendance records could help an operations manager organise recurring onboarding, subject to testing.. Coaching prompt: how does the learner justify name the buyer job? Watch for: Relying on generic words such as best-in-class.

  4. Small-scale demand experiments

    Demonstrate: A pilot target is 10 qualified enquiries from 200 visits; if only two arrive, reassess the offer or targeting.. Coaching prompt: how does the learner justify choose a risky assumption? Watch for: Moving the success threshold after viewing results.

Answer key and rationale

  1. An ICP principally describes: Correct option: Target organisation/customer characteristics. Review the module "Niches, ICPs and personas": A niche denotes a market segment. An ideal customer profile (ICP) specifies the attributes of likely-fit customers or organisations. A persona describes the circumstances, objectives and obstacles of a representative decision maker. All require evidence.
  2. Which is evidence of an actual buyer objection? Correct option: A neutral buyer interview. Review the module "Market research with source controls": Triangulate interviews, search signals, procurement patterns and competitors. Date sources, track who was sampled, separate observed facts from hypotheses and avoid collecting personal data without purpose.
  3. The strongest proposition links: Correct option: Customer need, outcome and proof. Review the module "Value propositions and buyer jobs": Translate a relevant customer job into a defensible business outcome, with features acting as evidence rather than substituting for customer value.
  4. What prevents hindsight bias? Correct option: Predefined measures and stop rules. Review the module "Small-scale demand experiments": Define a falsifiable hypothesis, test design, success threshold and stop rule before exposure. A consent-aware interest form, landing prototype or structured buyer interviews can validate interest without selling a nonexistent product.
  5. A source register should record: Correct option: Date and provenance. Apply the principles and controls in Niche & Audience Research; verify calculations and permissions before deciding.
  6. An AI-drafted persona is best described as: Correct option: A hypothesis to validate. Apply the principles and controls in Niche & Audience Research; verify calculations and permissions before deciding.
  7. Employee count is an example of: Correct option: Firmographic information. Apply the principles and controls in Niche & Audience Research; verify calculations and permissions before deciding.
  8. An ICP disqualifier serves to: Correct option: Flag poor-fit prospects. Apply the principles and controls in Niche & Audience Research; verify calculations and permissions before deciding.

Authenticity and lab marking

Review the learner's workbook and request a walkthrough. Ask the student to respond to a changed hypothetical scenario; note corrections and verify the original work. Score technical 30, evidence 25, governance 25 and communication 20. Flag critical personal-data disclosure or deceptive claims for remediation irrespective of average score.

Training service improvement

Record learner feedback, assessment problems, inaccessible materials, obsolete legal or vendor references, an owner for corrective action, and the verification date. Do not award an Academy credential through client-only practice quiz results.
