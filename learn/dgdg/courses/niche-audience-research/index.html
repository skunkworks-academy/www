Niche & Audience Research

Build evidence-backed buyer segmentation and validate demand.

Professional context

An SME cybersecurity-awareness trainer needs to distinguish the programme purchaser from its end learners.

Measurable outcomes

  • Define a niche, ICP and persona
  • Collect traceable research evidence
  • Translate pain points into value statements
  • Design a controlled demand validation test

Study duration includes independent research, practical construction, review and corrective work. Foundation modules require no paid campaign accounts; practitioner and advanced entries benefit from previous DGDG units or equivalent competence.

Structured learning modules

Lesson 1 / 4

Niches, ICPs and personas

A niche denotes a market segment. An ideal customer profile (ICP) specifies the attributes of likely-fit customers or organisations. A persona describes the circumstances, objectives and obstacles of a representative decision maker. All require evidence.

Open lesson →
Lesson 2 / 4

Market research with source controls

Triangulate interviews, search signals, procurement patterns and competitors. Date sources, track who was sampled, separate observed facts from hypotheses and avoid collecting personal data without purpose.

Open lesson →
Lesson 3 / 4

Value propositions and buyer jobs

Translate a relevant customer job into a defensible business outcome, with features acting as evidence rather than substituting for customer value.

Lesson 4 / 4

Small-scale demand experiments

Define a falsifiable hypothesis, test design, success threshold and stop rule before exposure. A consent-aware interest form, landing prototype or structured buyer interviews can validate interest without selling a nonexistent product.

Assessment policy

Four guided lessons, a practice assessment and a practical evidence assignment support each course. An 80% quiz threshold is a formative readiness indicator, not a certificate. A qualified assessor reviews the project against technical accuracy (30%), reproducible evidence (25%), ethics, security and privacy (25%), and communication/improvement (20%). Proposed practical threshold: 70/100 and no critical deceptive claim, privacy or integrity failure. Learner identity and awards require a controlled LMS process.
