DGDG-102 · practical activity

Applied Lab — Niche & Audience Research

Build and document a reusable workplace artefact; use fictional or appropriately anonymised data.

Scenario brief

An SME cybersecurity-awareness trainer needs to distinguish the programme purchaser from its end learners.

Tools and prerequisites

  • Spreadsheet or CSV editor
  • Browser and document editor
  • Course workbook template
  • Synthetic dataset and source register; no real ad spend is necessary

Six-step execution guide

  1. Task 1: Specify one ICP and two buyer personas. Record outputs, assumptions and verification evidence.
  2. Task 2: Record five dated research sources or clearly labelled simulated interview prompts. Record outputs, assumptions and verification evidence.
  3. Task 3: Rank purchase triggers and objections. Record outputs, assumptions and verification evidence.
  4. Task 4: Compare three alternative solutions. Record outputs, assumptions and verification evidence.
  5. Task 5: Draft and critique the value proposition. Record outputs, assumptions and verification evidence.
  6. Task 6: Define a test and decision rule. Record outputs, assumptions and verification evidence.

Deliverable package

  • Completed worksheet and traceable sources
  • One-page implementation or campaign architecture
  • Annotated calculations and evidence screenshots, where relevant
  • Risks, privacy/security decisions and required approvals
  • A 150–250-word reflection and one iteration plan

Scoring rubric (100 points)

CriterionWeightEvidence
Technical validity and correct calculations30Methods and figures can be reproduced
Evidence and provenance25Sources, assumptions and revision trail
Ethics, privacy, security25Claims, permissions and risk controls checked
Communication and improvement20Clear result and justified next actions

Pass proposal: at least 70/100, no critical deceptive-claim, privacy or integrity failure, and an assessor-verified walkthrough. This static page does not accept certified submissions.

Debrief questions

What assumption was least supported? What is the strongest evidence of buyer value? What incident would force a pause? What would you change after one week of measured feedback?
