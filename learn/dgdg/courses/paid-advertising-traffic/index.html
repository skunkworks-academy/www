Professional context

An Academy has a hypothetical R6,000 ad budget for a professional workshop.

Measurable outcomes

  • Set outcome-aligned campaign objectives
  • Choose relevant targeting and exclusions
  • Forecast CPL and CAC scenarios
  • Apply spend, brand and approval controls

Study duration includes independent research, practical construction, review and corrective work. Foundation modules require no paid campaign accounts; practitioner and advanced entries benefit from previous DGDG units or equivalent competence.

Structured learning modules

Lesson 1 / 4

Spend and acquisition economics

Define qualified-lead and customer outcomes before selecting bidding. CPL is spend divided by leads and CAC is spend divided by acquired customers. All forecasts depend on assumptions.

Lesson 2 / 4

Keywords, audience and exclusions

Match search queries and channel audiences to real buyer intent, exclude irrelevant terms, and inspect advertising policies for targeting limits and sensitive classifications.

Lesson 3 / 4

Creative and destination alignment

Ad copy must match the actual destination, eligibility, pricing context and substantiated outcomes. Landing accessibility and page performance influence buyer confidence.

Campaign controls and release gates

Assessment policy

Four guided lessons, a practice assessment and a practical evidence assignment support each course. An 80% quiz threshold is a formative readiness indicator, not a certificate. A qualified assessor reviews the project against technical accuracy (30%), reproducible evidence (25%), ethics, security and privacy (25%), and communication/improvement (20%). Proposed practical threshold: 70/100 and no critical deceptive claim, privacy or integrity failure. Learner identity and awards require a controlled LMS process.
