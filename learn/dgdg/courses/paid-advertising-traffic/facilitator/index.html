Facilitator and Assessor Notes

Paid Advertising & Traffic

Pre-delivery quality checks

Assess learner needs, access accommodations, prerequisite competence and the business scenario. Use approved fictional data. Keep feedback respectful, specific, dated and traceable to course objectives.

Delivery and questioning plan

  1. Spend and acquisition economics

    Demonstrate: R6,000 at assumed R60 CPL suggests 100 leads only if performance meets the assumption; customers depend on downstream conversion.. Coaching prompt: how does the learner justify set business outcome? Watch for: Treating revenue forecasts as guarantees.

  2. Keywords, audience and exclusions

    Demonstrate: Target training-related queries while excluding unrelated free software download intent.. Coaching prompt: how does the learner justify group keywords? Watch for: Selecting invasive sensitive-attribute targeting.

  3. Creative and destination alignment

    Demonstrate: An ad saying five-day course links to a syllabus accurately describing five-day delivery and prerequisites.. Coaching prompt: how does the learner justify draft three ad versions? Watch for: Contradictory promises between ad and landing page.

  4. Campaign controls and release gates

    Demonstrate: A controlled pilot has maximum R6,000 spend, documented owner, conversion-test evidence and stop rule when lead quality drops.. Coaching prompt: how does the learner justify create pre-launch checklist? Watch for: Activating unbounded spend with broken tracking.

Answer key and rationale

  1. CAC means: Correct option: Cost per acquired customer. Review the module "Spend and acquisition economics": Define qualified-lead and customer outcomes before selecting bidding. CPL is spend divided by leads and CAC is spend divided by acquired customers. All forecasts depend on assumptions.
  2. Negative keywords: Correct option: Exclude irrelevant searches. Review the module "Keywords, audience and exclusions": Match search queries and channel audiences to real buyer intent, exclude irrelevant terms, and inspect advertising policies for targeting limits and sensitive classifications.
  3. Message match means: Correct option: Consistent ad and destination offer. Review the module "Creative and destination alignment": Ad copy must match the actual destination, eligibility, pricing context and substantiated outcomes. Landing accessibility and page performance influence buyer confidence.
  4. Before launch, verify: Correct option: Budgets, tracking, claims and policy. Review the module "Campaign controls and release gates": Apply hard spend limits, alerts, naming standards, approvals, tracking tests and incident stop actions. Record changes and evaluate whether qualified results justify continuing.
  5. R2,000 spend for 20 leads produces CPL: Correct option: R100. Apply the principles and controls in Paid Advertising & Traffic; verify calculations and permissions before deciding.
  6. An ad forecast is: Correct option: A scenario based on assumptions. Apply the principles and controls in Paid Advertising & Traffic; verify calculations and permissions before deciding.
  7. A campaign change log provides: Correct option: Accountability and root-cause evidence. Apply the principles and controls in Paid Advertising & Traffic; verify calculations and permissions before deciding.
  8. If an ad is policy-rejected: Correct option: Review policy and correct/appeal lawfully. Apply the principles and controls in Paid Advertising & Traffic; verify calculations and permissions before deciding.

Authenticity and lab marking

Review the learner's workbook and request a walkthrough. Ask the student to respond to a changed hypothetical scenario; note corrections and verify the original work. Score technical 30, evidence 25, governance 25 and communication 20. Flag critical personal-data disclosure or deceptive claims for remediation irrespective of average score.

Training service improvement

Record learner feedback, assessment problems, inaccessible materials, obsolete legal or vendor references, an owner for corrective action, and the verification date. Do not award an Academy credential through client-only practice quiz results.
