Academic release gate

  1. Subject-matter reviewer approves technical accuracy and examples.
  2. Legal and privacy reviewer verifies jurisdictional claims and learner data collection.
  3. Accessibility evaluator completes WCAG 2.2 AA test matrix.
  4. Academic lead approves assessment validity and marking consistency.
  5. Operations integrates server-side enrolment, evidence retention and certificate approvals; never rely solely on browser scores.
  6. Quality lead records the revision, date, owner, learner feedback and next review trigger.
