DGDG Programme Guide

Delivery, competence objectives, learner services, assessment and verification

Programme context

Audience: professional learners, marketers, growth and sales teams, entrepreneurs and learning partners. Delivery: 10 guided self-paced courses supported by instructor moderation. Simulated data and offline spreadsheet tools are acceptable; live spend, scraping or unsolicited outreach are not required.

Learning and assessment cycle

  1. Check prerequisites, accessibility and relevant prior experience; document accommodations.
  2. Complete lesson readings, worked examples, guided tasks and reflection.
  3. Use eight-question formative quiz with recommended 80% threshold; review explanations and retry.
  4. Complete practical portfolio. Assessor scores technical 30, evidence 25, ethics/privacy 25, communication 20.
  5. Target ≥70/100 with no critical integrity failure; record human feedback and remediation.
  6. Submit identity-verified outcome to the approved Academy learner management system before credential review.

Accessibility and inclusion

Provide readable responsive web pages, visible keyboard focus, semantic form controls and print-ready alternatives. Offer accommodation for learner needs and test with keyboard and assistive technology before launch.

Evaluation and improvement

Academic reviewers should examine learner outcomes, lab rubrics, drop-off, quiz item difficulty, instructor comments and complaints. Record approved changes and conduct annual or event-driven content review. Marketing-law claims, pricing and vendor interfaces require independent currency checking.
